最终提示词：

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide key art for a dark fantasy dragon-themed chat application's dramatic left-hand illustrated panel and full-bleed background.
Primary request: An ORIGINAL ancient western dragon, in the punchy stylized anime-meets-American-comic visual language of Marvel Rivals hero splash illustrations: very strong silhouette, exaggerated heroic shapes, razor-sharp graphic plane changes, bold ink contours, painterly cel shading, dynamic perspective and confident limited halftone accents. Original creature design, no existing game character or logo.
Subject: A majestic colossal obsidian-and-crimson western dragon with crown-like ivory horns, angular plated face, luminous molten orange eye, bronze-gold jaw armor and volcanic fissures between black scales. Its head and chest turn in three-quarter view toward the right, long neck curved, one massive claw gripping broken black basalt at the lower left. A red wing forms a diagonal backdrop and ember ribbons frame its silhouette. Intelligent, regal and intimidating rather than a generic monster.
Composition: Landscape 3:2 composition. Keep the dragon's complete identifiable head between x=14% and x=43%, y=12% and y=46%; dragon body and wing occupy the LEFT 50-55% of the canvas. The RIGHT 45% must remain predominantly very dark charcoal with atmospheric smoke and sparse ember sparks, low detail, suitable behind a reading interface. At bottom left include dark negative space for typography that will be added in code. Nothing cropped through the face or horns. No text or interface baked into the image.
Lighting: Fierce warm vermilion and gold rim light with subtle cold slate-blue shadows; clear graphic separation, rich black planes, luminous eyes, selective saturated highlights.
Background: Ruined dragon citadel implied with a few angular silhouettes through smoke. Moody but not muddy, polished premium comic-game concept art.
Constraints: Not photorealistic; no photographic textures, no glossy 3D render, no chibi proportions, no human characters, no typography, no symbols resembling a brand logo, no UI, no border, no watermark. Deliver one wide complete illustration.
```
