Design direction name: 蜷眠守卷 / Moonlit Bookkeeper.
Give the theme a quiet companion and a sleeping guardian landscape.
DESKTOP: remove the current spread-wing dragon. A beautifully proportioned silver dragon sleeps curled on a small closed, worn midnight-blue book on the rocky ledge outside the far-right end of the composer. Its folded wings wrap naturally around its back, tail loosely loops around the book spine, relaxed chin rests on its forepaws. Mature elegant anatomy, a graceful silhouette, restrained scale texture. At the logical 1780×883 viewport, foreground dragon+book fits approximately 190px wide by 135px high in the bottom-right periphery, no wing rising into prose and no claw crossing the send circle. Its highlights are narrow, matte and subdued. It should feel like an inhabitant quietly resting, not a trophy on display.
DISTANT ENVIRONMENT: a giant ancient dragon sleeps along the distant far-right mountain crest, its long low back and folded wing read almost as part of the rock silhouette. Show the noble profile lightly outlined by mist, body largely lost in indigo haze. No bright eye. Its huge implied length communicates age and scale while low contrast keeps it peripheral. Do not run a dragon outline horizontally under the text.
MOBILE: replace the current 100px spread-wing ornament with a VERY SMALL sleeping Q-version of the same silver dragon, approximately 68px wide by 44px high at a 393×852 viewport. Curl it into a compact crescent shape, small paws tucked under its muzzle, tiny amethyst wing folds, fine matte silver scales. Place it at the far-right end of the existing quick-reply/composer rim area, near the bottom-right, entirely outside the writing field and the send control. Use that existing peripheral gap; no extra full-width shelf. It must not overlap the story or its last lines. The giant sleeping dragon is only a faint deep-background ridge at the upper-right edge, mostly obscured by cool cloud, with very little contrast under prose.
Emotional tone: intimate moonlit reading, a faithful dragon quietly asleep beside the reader. The plain text remains the first visual focus. No loud glows or theatrical wings. Render both complete app views with realistic long-form reading content.
